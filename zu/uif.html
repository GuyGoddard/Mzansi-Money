UIF — Isikhwama Sokuqinisekisa Ukungasebenzi

Uxhaso lwakho lwezezimali uma ulahlekelwa umsebenzi, uya ekhaya ukubeletha, ugula noma ubhekana nezinye izimo.

Yini i-UIF?

UIF is a government fund that both you and your employer contribute to every month. If you lose your job (not by choice), you can claim back up to Izinsuku ezingama-238 of financial support.

I-UIF — Imibuzo Ejwayelekile

Izinkokhelo ze-UIF zithatha izinsuku eziyi-4 kuya ku-8 ngemuva kokufaka isicelo esigcwele ku-uFiling. Isikhathi sixhomeke ekutheni umqashi wakho ubeke ifomu lakhe le-UI-19 noma cha nasekupheleni kwamagratjhi akho. Uma kugunyaziwe, imali ifakwa ngqo ku-akhawunti yakho yasebhange.

Yebo. Abasebenzi basendlini (izincekuzi, abalimi, abagcini bezingane) abasebenza ngaphezulu kwamahora angu-24 ngenyanga baklanyiwe i-UIF. Umqashi wabo kufanele ababhalisele i-UIF futhi arhole i-1% emholdini ngenyanga.

"Thumele Kum-Assessor" kusho ukuthi amagratjhi akho e-UIF atholakele futhi manje ahlolwa um-assessor. Lokhu kujwayeleke ukuthi kuthatha izinsuku ezingu-5–15 zebhizinisi. Ngemuva kwalokho, isimo sakho kufanele sishintshele ku-"Assessed" bese ku-"Approved".

Isikhathi sakho senzuzo ye-UIF sixhomeke ekubeni kade unikela. Ngazo zonke izinsuku eziyi-4 osebenze ngazo njengomnikeli, uwina usuku olulodwa lwenzuzo ye-UIF. Usilinganiso oluphezulu izinsuku eziyi-365. Izinzuzo zokuzala zilinganiselwa ezinsukwini eziyi-121.
