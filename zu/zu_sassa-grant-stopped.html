SASSA Guide

Isibonelelo Sami se-SASSA Senqatshiwe — Yini Okufanele Ngiyenze

Uma isibonelelo sakho se-SASSA sinqatshiwe ngesiphazamiso noma ungalitholi imali yakho leli nyanga, ungaphazamiseki. Kunaizizathu ezijwayelekile ezibangela lokhu, futhi eziningi zingalunga. Lo mhlahlandlela uzokukhombisa ngalinye.

info

First step: Check your status before assuming anything is wrong. Sometimes there is a payment delay and your money will arrive within a few days.

Izizathu ezijwayelekile zokuqeda isibonelelo

assignment_turned_in
Awuqinisekisanga imininingwane yakho (ukuqinisekisa kabusha)

I-SASSA idinga abamukelwa izibonelelo ukuba baqinisekise imininingwane yabo njalo eminyakeni eyi-1 kuya ku-2. Uma uphuthile ukuqinisekisa kwakho, isibonelelo sakho sikhanyiselwa kuze ube uye. Shayela i-SASSA ukuthola ukuthi owakho uhlela nini.

trending_up
Ingeniso yakho noma impahla yakho kushintshile

Uma i-SASSA ithole ukuthi ingeniso yakho noma impahla yakho manje kudlula umkhawulo wohlobo lwesibonelelo sakho, izonqanda isibonelelo. Lokhu kungenzeka uma uthole umsebenzi omutsha, wamukele ifa, noma washada.

account_balance
Imininingwane ye-akhawunti yakho yasebhange ishintshile noma iyiphutha

Uma ushintshe amabhange noma inombolo ye-akhawunti yakho ishintshile, ukukhokha akukwazi ukudlula. Buyekeza imininingwane yakho yasebhange eSikhungweni se-SASSA esinaso noma ku-www.sassa.gov.za.

local_hospital
Usesibhedlela noma endaweni yokunakekelwa

Uma wangeniswa esibhedlela esixhaswa nguhulumeni noma endaweni yokunakekelwa isikhathi esingaphezu kwezinyanga ezi-3, isibonelelo sakho siyaqedwa. Singabuyiselwa uma uphuma.

security
Kwakunenkomba yokudlwengulwa ku-akhawunti yakho

Uma omunye umuntu wazama ukufinyelela isibonelelo sakho ngokukhwabanisa, i-SASSA ingase ibe ikhóbóze i-akhawunti yakho ukukuvikela. Vakashela iSikhungo se-SASSA nedokumente lakho lobunikazi ukuxazulula lokhu.

person_remove
Izimo zakho zishintshile (isib. i-Child Support Grant)

Ngesikhathi se-Child Support Grant, uma ingane isiphenduke iminyaka eli-18, ishonile, noma ingasekho phansi kokunakekela kwakho, isibonelelo siyama. Ngesikhathi se-Disability Grant, isimo sakho singase siphinde sihlolwe.

Yini okufanele uyenze manje

1
Qala uhlole isimo sakho se-SASSA

Iya ku-srd.sassa.gov.za noma shayela inombolo engu-0800 60 10 11 ukuhlola isimo sakho. Ngezinye izikhathi ukuphuza nje, hhayi ukuqedwa okuphakade.

2
Hamba eHhovisi le-SASSA eliseduze nawe

Letha ikhadi lakho lobunikazi. Buza ngokukhethekile: "Kungani isibonelelo sami sinqatshiwe?" Kufanele bakutshele isizathu ngokubhala.

3
Lungisa inkinga ngokusebenzisa isizathu

Buyekeza imininingwane yasebhange uma yiyo inkinga. Letha ubufakazi bwengeniso uma kuhlolwa ingeniso. Buza ngesikhathi sokuqinisekisa kabusha uma kudingeka.

4
Cela incwadi eqinisekisa icala lakho

Thola konke ngokubhala. Cela inombolo yesireferensi. Lokhu kukuvikela uma kukhona impikiswano.

5
Faka isikhalo uma ungavumelani nokuqwashisa

Uma i-SASSA inqanda isibonelelo sakho ngokungalungile, unezinsuku ezingama-90 zokufaka isikhalo. Cela ifomu lesikhalo eSikhungweni se-SASSA esinaso.

warning

Important: SASSA will not automatically restart your grant. You must go in person or call them. The sooner you do it, the sooner your grant restarts.

Imibuzo ejwayelekile

Kuncike kezizathu. Uma i-SASSA inqanda isibonelelo sakho ngephutha, kufanele uthole ukukhokhelwa emuva ezinyangeni eziphuthekelwe. Uma kunqatshwa ngenxa yoshintsho lwangempela ezimweni zakho, ngeke uthole ukukhokhelwa emuva.

Ikhadi lakho lingase liphelile, likhonyiwe, noma imininingwane yakho ingase ishintshile. Hamba eHhovisi le-SASSA eliseduze nawe noma e-Post Office nedokumente lakho lobunikazi ukuthola ikhadi elisha noma ukudubula elikhona.

Cela ilungu lomndeni ukuya egameni lakho nencwadi yokunika igunya kanye nekhophi yedokumente lakho lobunikazi. Noma shayela i-SASSA ku-0800 60 10 11 ubacele ukuba balungiselele ukuvakashela ekhaya.
