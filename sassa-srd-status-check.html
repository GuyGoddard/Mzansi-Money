🔍 How to Check Your SRD Status (3 Official Methods)
🌐

Method 1: Official SRD Portal (recommended)

The fastest and most detailed method. Shows your monthly result and payment date.

👉 Go to: srd.sassa.gov.za/sc19/status

Enter your 13-digit ID number and the cellphone number you used to apply, then tap Check Status. You need data or Wi-Fi.

💬

Method 2: WhatsApp

Works well on a smartphone. SASSA's chatbot guides you through the check.

👉 WhatsApp: 082 046 8553

Send "Hi" to start. You'll need your ID number. Uses data but WhatsApp is zero-rated on some networks.

📞

Method 3: USSD (no data needed)

Works on any phone including basic feature phones. No data or internet needed.

👉 Dial: *120*69277#

Follow the prompts. Standard USSD rates may apply depending on your network.

📊 What Does Your Status Mean?

✅ Approved

Your eligibility has been confirmed for this month. Your payment will be processed on your scheduled pay date. Check the portal for your specific pay date within the 24–30 June window.

⏳ Pending / Under Review

SASSA is still verifying your income, banking details, or ID against Home Affairs and SARS records. This is normal early in the month. Check again in a few days — it usually updates before the payment window.

❌ Declined

Your application was rejected for this month. Common reasons: income detected above R624/month, UIF registration found, government employment record, or existing SASSA grant. You can appeal within 90 days at srd.sassa.gov.za. Each month's decline can be appealed separately.

🔄 Payment Processing / Bank Reprocessing

Your payment was approved but there was a banking issue — your account may have been closed, frozen, or the name doesn't match. Update your banking details on the SRD portal. Reprocessing typically takes 7–14 working days.

🛠️ Common Problems & Fixes

Status is "Approved" but no money has arrived

Wait until the end of the 24–30 June window. Banks take 2–3 business days to clear the payment. If nothing has arrived by 3 July, call SASSA on 0800 60 10 11 (free) or visit your nearest SASSA office.

Status says "declined" but I have no income

SASSA's system may have found a record that doesn't reflect your situation. Submit an appeal at srd.sassa.gov.za explaining your circumstances. You can appeal within 90 days of a declined result. Each month's decision is assessed separately.

I can't log in — "ID and phone number don't match"

You must use the exact cellphone number used when you originally applied. If you changed your number, you need to update your details at a SASSA office with your original ID document.

Payment was sent but bounced back

Your bank account may be closed or frozen, or the account name doesn't match your ID. Check with your bank for reversed transactions. Update your banking details on the SRD portal at srd.sassa.gov.za.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
When are SASSA SRD R370 payments for June 2026?
SRD R370 payments for June 2026 are processed in rolling batches between 24 and 30 June 2026. Your personal payment date may differ — check the official SRD portal at srd.sassa.gov.za for your specific date.
Is the SRD R370 grant still active in 2026?
Yes. The SRD R370 grant has been extended through 31 March 2027 with a dedicated R36.4 billion budget allocation. Despite political changes in the Social Development ministry in May 2026, payments continue normally.
Will the SRD grant amount increase in 2026?
No. The SRD R370 amount has remained frozen at R370 for the third consecutive year, despite other grants receiving 3.5% CPI increases from April 2026. There is no confirmed increase for 2026/27.
How often does my SRD status get checked?
SASSA re-verifies your eligibility every single month. Each payment cycle, the system cross-checks your details against Home Affairs, SARS income data (the R624 means test), and your bank account. A previous approval does not guarantee the next month's payment.
What is the income threshold for the SRD grant?
You must earn less than R624 per month to qualify for the SRD R370 grant. SASSA checks this against your bank account transactions and SARS records each month.
How do I appeal a declined SRD status?
Visit srd.sassa.gov.za, log in with your ID and phone number, and select the declined month to appeal. You have 90 days from the date of the decline to submit an appeal. The Independent Tribunal for Social Assistance Appeals (ITSAA) handles SRD appeals.
