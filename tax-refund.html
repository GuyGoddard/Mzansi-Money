3 Complete your return — include medical aid, pension and travel if applicable
4 Submit — SARS pays refunds within 72 hours of an accepted return

Tax Refund FAQs

If your employer deducted more PAYE than your actual tax liability — due to medical aid credits, pension contributions, or a travel allowance — SARS will owe you a refund. File your ITR12 on eFiling and SARS processes refunds within 72 hours of an accepted return.

SARS typically pays refunds within 72 hours of accepting your tax return. Ensure your banking details are correct on eFiling. Complex cases can take up to 3 weeks if SARS requests supporting documents.

Four main deductions increase your refund: pension/RA contributions (up to 27.5% of income), medical aid tax credits (R376/month per person), travel allowance deductions if you use your own vehicle for work, and home office expenses if your employer requires remote work.

Yes. SARS will not automatically pay a refund — you must file your ITR12 return on eFiling or the SARS MobiApp. SARS may send you an auto-assessment SMS in July — if it shows a refund and you accept it, the refund is paid without filing manually.
