Izigaba zesilinganiso sokhrediti eNingizimu Afrika

ENingizimu Afrika, isilinganiso sivame ukuba phakathi kuka-300–850. Isilinganiso sango-2026 singu-615.

UkuBuyekezwa Kwezikweletu — Kuyini lokho?

If you're struggling with debt, debt review (debt counselling) restructures your payments to fit your budget and protects your assets from repossession.

One reduced monthly payment instead of many
Protection from legal action by creditors
Takes 3–5 years, then you get a clearance certificate
You cannot take on new credit during debt review
Important: Only use NCR-registered debt counsellors. Verify at ncr.org.za or call 0860 627 627.

Isilinganiso Sokhrediti — Imibuzo Ejwayelekile

Ukuthuthukisa amapointi esikweletu kuthatha isikhathi. Izilisti ezibi njengezinkokhelo ezingakhokhelwanga zihlala iminyaka emibili; izahlulelo iminyaka emihlanu. Ngezinkokhelo ezikhokhelwa ngesikhathi, abantu abaningi babona ukuthuthuka kwangempela phakathi kwezinyanga eziyi-6 kuya ku-12.

Cha. Ukuhlola umbiko wakho wesikweletu ngokwakho kubizwa nge-soft enquiry futhi akuphazamisi amapointi akho nhlobo. Zihlola umbiko wakho okungenani kanye ngonyaka — akubizi lutho.

Amabhange amaningi aseNingizimu Afrika adinga amapointi angu-620-650 yemalimboleka yendlu. Amapointi angaphezu kuka-650 akunika amathuba angu-85% okwamukela. Amapointi angaphezu kuka-700 angakunikeza izinga elincane lenzalo.

Landa umbiko wakho wamahhala wesikweletu evela ku-TransUnion, Experian, noma XDS. Uma uthole iphutha, xhumana ngqo nebhiro lesikweletu nophumela. Ibhiro kufanele lihlole phakathi kwezinsuku ezingama-20 zebhizinisi.
