Ukufaka eFiling — Inyathelo Ngenyathelo

1
Go to efiling.sars.gov.za

Or download the SARS MobiApp. Never use unofficial websites.

2
Bhalisela okanye ngena

You need your SA ID, tax reference number and a valid email address.

3
Click "Returns" then "Income Tax"

Khetha i-ITR12 (abantu ngabanye) ngonyaka werhafu ofanelekileyo.

4
Gcwalisa iinkcukacha zakho

Employment income (IRP5), medical aid, retirement contributions, travel allowance.

5
Faka uze ulinde

SARS processes refunds within 72 hours. Assessments may take up to 3 weeks.

Irhafu — Imibuzo Ebuzwa Kaninzi

Ukuba umqeshi wakho ebekhe i-PAYE engaphezulu kokuba yintlawulo yakho yerhafu yangempela — ngenxa yegalelo eliphezulu lezempilo, ihlomelo lohlala-phantsi, okanye ukukhubazeka — i-SARS iya kukubuyisela imali. Faka ingxelo yakho kwi-eFiling; ukuba kukweleta imali, ibonakala njengesamba esinegethivu.

I-SARS ibeka izohlwayo zolawulo ukungafaki ngexesha, ziqala ku-R250 ngenyanga yanda ngokwemali yakho. Izohlwayo zinokuqokelana kude kube ku-R16,000 ngenyanga. I-SARS inganezela nenzala. Kusoloko kulungile ukufaka nokuba awukwazi ukuhlawula.

Ewe, kodwa imithetho iyaqinisekiswa. Kufuneka usebenzise igumbi elinikeleweyo kuphela emsebenzini, umqeshi wakho kufuneka akufune ukusebenza ekhaya, kwaye akufuneki uhlaziswe ngezindleko. Ungathetha inxalenye yentlawulo yokuqeshwa, umbane, nezindleko zokulungiswa.

I-SARS auto-assessment yeyona i-SARS ibala ingxelo yakho ngokuzenzekelayo isebenzisa idatha evela kumqeshi wakho, isikhungo sezempilo, nezicwangciso. Ufumana i-SMS ngo-Julayi kwaye uneentsuku ezingama-40 zebhizinisi zokuyamkela okanye ukuyilungisa kwi-eFiling.
