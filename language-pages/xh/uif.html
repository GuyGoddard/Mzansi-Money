UIF — Igugu Lokuqinisekiswa Kokungasebenzi

Ukhuseleko lwakho lwemali xa ulahlekelwa ngumsebenzi, uya kwimayela yokukhulelwa, ugula okanye ubhekana nezinye iimeko.

Yintoni i-UIF?

UIF is a government fund that both you and your employer contribute to every month. If you lose your job (not by choice), you can claim back up to Iimihla eziyi-238 of financial support.

I-UIF — Imibuzo Ebuzwa Kaninzi

Iintlawulo ze-UIF zithatha iiveki eziyi-4 ukuya kwi-8 emva kokufaka isicelo esigcwele kwi-uFiling. Umxholo uxhomekeke ekubeni umqeshi wakho unikele ifomu le-UI-19 kwaye imiqulu yakho iphelele. Xa kuvunyiwe, imali ifakwa ngqo kwi-akhawunti yakho yasebhange.

Ewe. Abasebenzi basendlini (abalondolozi, abalimi, abanakekeli babantwana) abasebenzayo ngaphezulu kwamahora angama-24 ngenyanga bakhushelwa yi-UIF. Umqeshi wabo kufuneka ababhalisele i-UIF kwaye aphungule i-1% kumvuzo ngenyanga.

"Thumele kuM-Assessor" kuthetha ukuba imiqulu yakho ye-UIF ifunyenwe kwaye ngoku iyahlolwa ngum-assessor. Oku kujolise ukuthatha iintsuku ezingama-5–15 zebhizinisi. Emva koko, imeko yakho kufuneka itshintshele ku-"Assessed" emva koko ku-"Approved".

Ubude bolonwabo lwakho lwe-UIF luxhomekeke ekubeni kade unikelile. Ngazo zonke iintsuku ezine ezisebenzileyo njengomsebenzisi, ufumana usuku olunye lwenzuzo ye-UIF. Usilinganiso oluninzi ziintsuku eziyi-365. Izibonelelo zokuzala zilinganiselwa kwiintsuku eziyi-121.
