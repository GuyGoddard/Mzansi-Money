UkuBuyekezwa Kwamatyala — Yintoni?

If you're struggling with debt, debt review (debt counselling) restructures your payments to fit your budget and protects your assets from repossession.

One reduced monthly payment instead of many
Protection from legal action by creditors
Takes 3–5 years, then you get a clearance certificate
You cannot take on new credit during debt review
Important: Only use NCR-registered debt counsellors. Verify at ncr.org.za or call 0860 627 627.

Isi-Score Sekredi — Imibuzo Ebuzwa Kaninzi

Ukuphucula amanqaku esikweletu kuthatha ixesha. Izilisti ezimbi njengezintlawulo ezisentshonalanga ziqhuba iminyaka emibini; izigwebo iminyaka emihlanu. Ngeentlawulo ezihlawulwa ngexesha, abantu abaningi babona ukulungiswa okubalulekileyo phakathi kweeveki ezingama-6 ukuya ku-12.

Hayi. Ukujonga ingxelo yakho yesikweletu ngokwakho kubizwa nge-soft enquiry kwaye akunamdla kumanqaku akho. Jonga ingxelo yakho ubuncinane kanye ngonyaka — akukhonki nto.

Abantu abaningi abasebenzisa iibhanki zaseMzantsi Afrika badinga amanqaku angama-620-650 wemalimboleka yendlu. Amanqaku angaphezulu kwe-650 akunika amathuba angama-85% okwamkelwa.

Khuphela ingxelo yakho yasimahla yonyaka yesikweletu evela ku-TransUnion, Experian, okanye XDS. Ukuba ufumana impazamo, qhagamshelana ngqo nebhiro lesikweletu ngobungqina. Ibhiro kufuneka lihlole phakathi kweentsuku eziyi-20 zebhizinisi.
