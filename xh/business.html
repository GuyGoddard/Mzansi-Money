Ubhaliso Lweshishini Nenkxaso Yemali

Bhalisa ishishini lakho ne-CIPC, fumana inkxaso kahulumeni, uhlale uhambelana nentlawulo.

Amanyathelo Ali-4 Alula Okubhalisa Ishishini Lakho

1
Gcina Igama Lakho

Cinga ngeenketho zegama ezi-4. Hlola ukufumaneka ubhalisele ku-bizportal.gov.za nge-R50. Iyasebenza iinyanga ezi-6.

2
Bhalisa ne-CIPC

Gcwalisa ubhaliso ku-cipc.co.za okanye ku-bizportal.gov.za. Ixabiso: R125. Ufuna i-SA ID nobungqina bokuhlala.

Open CIPC Checklist chevron_right
3
Fumana Inombolo Yakho Yerhafu (SARS)

Inkampani yakho ibhalisiwa ngokuzenzekelayo kwirhafu xa i-CIPC igunyaza ubhaliso lwakho. Bhalisela i-VAT xa ingeniso idlula u-R1M.

4
Vula I-Akhawunti Yeshishini

Ahlula imali yobuqu nemali yeshishini. I-FNB Business Zero, i-Capitec Business ne-Nedbank zinikela ii-akhawunti zamahhala.

Irhafu Nokumana Kwamashishini

I-PAYE — Hlawula Njengoko Urhola

Ukuba uhlawula abasebenzi, bhalisela i-PAYE ufake i-EMP201 nyanga yonke ku-SARS.

I-VAT — Irhafu Yeentengo

Register for VAT when your turnover exceeds R1 million/year. You can voluntarily register from R50,000/year. VAT rate is 15%.

Iminikelo ye-UIF ne-SDL

Bhalisela bonke abasebenzi e-UIF (1% + 1%). Hlawula i-Skills Development Levy ukuba imivuzo idlula u-R500,000/ngonyaka.

Irhafu Yesikhashana

Ukuba ingeniso enethabiso idlula u-R30,000, ungumhlawuli werhafu yesikhashana. Hlawula ngo-Agasti nango-Februwari.

Iingxelo Zonyaka ku-CIPC

Faka iingxelo zonyaka ngaphakathi kweentsuku zemisebenzi eziyi-30. Ukungahambelani kukhokelela ekucinyisweni.
