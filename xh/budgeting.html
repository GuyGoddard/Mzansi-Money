1

2

3

Iistokveli zisebenza njani

Ngaphezu kwezigidi eziyi-11 zabantu baseMzantsi Afrika bathabatha inxaxheba kumastokveli.

✓ General stokvel: Monthly contributions, one member receives all at a time
✓ Grocery stokvel: Pool money to buy food in bulk at year-end
✓ Investment stokvel: Pool money into interest-bearing accounts
warning

⚠️ Qaphela amaqhinga eemali-mboleko

Unregistered lenders often charge 30–100% monthly interest. This is illegal under the National Credit Act. Always check that a lender is registered with the NCR at ncr.org.za or call 0860 627 627.

Isicwangciso Semali — Imibuzo Ebuzwa Kaninzi

Amacebo asebenzayo kakhulu: joyina istokveli ukonga ngokunyanzela; sebenzisa i-akhawunti yasebhange yasimahla (Capitec, TymeBank) ukukhepha iimali zenyanga; thenga ukutya ngokungaphezulu; sebenzisa i-laybye ezentweni ezinkulu esikhundleni sekhredithi; gcina imali kwi-akhawunti yokonga yasimahla.

Iinkonzo ze-BNPL zinokuba luncedo ukuba uhlawula phakathi nexesha elingenanzala, kodwa zinezingozi. Iinkonzo eziningi ze-BNPL zisebenza ngaphandle kwe-National Credit Act — okuthetha ukuba unezikhuselo ezimbalwa. Sebenzisa i-BNPL kuphela ukuthenga okuhlelelweyo onokukwazi ukukuhlawula.

Ukuqala istokveli: hlanganisa abantu abathembekileyo abangama-4 ukuya ku-12; vumana ngegalelo lenyanga abantu bonke abangalifumana; misela uhlobo lwentlawulo; misela umphathi-mali nonobhala; vula i-akhawunti yasebhange eyahlukileyo egameni leqela; kwaye ubhale isivumelwano esibhaliweyo abantu bonke abasayinayo. Bhalisela ku-NASASA kwi-nasasa.co.za.
