SASSA Guide

Isibonelelo Sami se-SASSA Siyekisiwe — Yintoni Ekufuneka Ndiyenze

Ukuba isibonelelo sakho se-SASSA siyekisiwe ngesiquphe okanye awuzange ufumane imali yakho le nyanga, musa ukoyika. Zikhona izizathu eziqhelekileyo ezibangela oku, kwaye uninzi lwayo lunokulunga. Lo mhlahlandlela ukukhokela kulo ngalinye.

info

First step: Check your status before assuming anything is wrong. Sometimes there is a payment delay and your money will arrive within a few days.

Izizathu eziqhelekileyo zokuyekiswa kwesibonelelo

assignment_turned_in
Awuzange uqinisekise iinkcukacha zakho (ukuqinisekisa kwakhona)

I-SASSA ifuna abafumani bezibonelelo ukuba baqinisekise iinkcukacha zabo rhoqo emva kweminyaka eyi-1 ukuya kwi-2. Ukuba uphosile isigqibo sakho sokuqinisekisa, isibonelelo sakho siyamiswa de uye. Tsalela i-SASSA umnxeba ukuze ufumanise nini owakho ubekelwe.

trending_up
Umvuzo wakho okanye izinto zakho zashintsha

Ukuba i-SASSA ifumanisa ukuba umvuzo wakho okanye izinto zakho ngoku zidlula umda wohlobo lwesibonelelo sakho, iza kuyekisa isibonelelo. Oku kunokwenzeka ukuba ufumene umsebenzi omtsha, wamkela ilifa, okanye watshatwa.

account_balance
Iinkcukacha ze-akhawunti yakho yasebhankini zashintsha okanye ziphosile

Ukuba utshintsha iibhanki okanye inombolo ye-akhawunti yakho itshintshile, intlawulo yakho ayikwazi ukudlula. Hlaziya iinkcukacha zakho zebhanki kwi-Ofisi ye-SASSA nayiphi na okanye ku-www.sassa.gov.za.

local_hospital
Usesibhedlela okanye kwisibonelelo sokunakekela

Ukuba wangeniswa esibhedlela esikhululekileyo ngurhulumente okanye kwisakhiwo sokunakekela ixesha elide ukuba zinyanga ezi-3, isibonelelo sakho siyamiswa. Sinokubuya ukusebenza xa uphuma.

security
Kwakukho uphawu lwentswela-bulungisa ku-akhawunti yakho

Ukuba omnye umntu wazama ukungena kwisibonelelo sakho ngentswela-bulungisa, i-SASSA ingathatha amanyathelo okulinda i-akhawunti yakho ukukuvikela. Tyelela i-Ofisi ye-SASSA nangalo lonke uxwebhu lwakho lobunikazi ukuze uxazulule oku.

person_remove
Iimeko zakho zashintsha (umz. i-Child Support Grant)

Ye-Child Support Grant, ukuba umntwana useneminyaka eli-18, edlule emhlabeni, okanye esengaphansi kokunakekela kwakho, isibonelelo siyeka. Ye-Disability Grant, imeko yakho inokuphinda ihlolwe.

Yintoni ekufuneka uyenze ngoku

1
Qala ujonge imeko yakho ye-SASSA

Yiya ku-srd.sassa.gov.za okanye tsalela umnxeba ku-0800 60 10 11 ukujonga imeko yakho. Ngamanye amaxesha kulibaziseka nje, akukhona ukuyekiswa okusisigxina.

2
Yiya kwiOfisi ye-SASSA ekufuphi nawe

Yisa uxwebhu lwakho lobunikazi. Buza ngokukodwa: "Yini na isibonelelo sami siyekisiwe?" Kufuneka bakuxelele isizathu ngokubhala.

3
Lungisa ingxaki ngokusebenzisa isizathu

Hlaziya iinkcukacha zebhanki ukuba yiyo ingxaki. Yisa ubungqina bomvuzo ukuba kuhlolwa umvuzo. Buza ngexesha lokuqinisekisa kwakhona ukuba kuyimfuneko.

4
Cela ileta equmisa ityala lakho

Fumana konke ngokubhala. Cela inombolo yesalathiso. Oku kukuvikela ukuba kukho ingxabano.

5
Faka isicelo-nkululeko ukuba awuvumelani neso sigqibo

Ukuba i-SASSA iyekisile isibonelelo sakho ngendlela engachanekanga, uneentsuku ezingama-90 zokufaka isicelo-nkululeko. Cela ifomu lokufaka isicelo kwi-Ofisi ye-SASSA nayiphi na.

warning

Important: SASSA will not automatically restart your grant. You must go in person or call them. The sooner you do it, the sooner your grant restarts.

Imibuzo eqhelekileyo

Kuxhomekeke kwisizathu. Ukuba i-SASSA iyekisile isibonelelo sakho ngempazamo, kufuneka ufumane intlawulo emva kwezinyanga eziphosekileyo. Ukuba yayiyekisiwe ngenxa yotshintsho lwangempela kwiimeko zakho, awuyi kufumana intlawulo emva.

Ikhadi lakho linokuba liphelile, likhonkxiwe, okanye iinkcukacha zakho zinokuba zishintshile. Yiya kwiOfisi ye-SASSA ekufuphi nawe okanye kwiPost Office noxwebhu lwakho lobunikazi ukufumana ikhadi elitsha okanye ukukhulula elikhoyo.

Cela ilungu losapho ukuba lihambe egameni lakho ngenxa yakho nencwadi yemvume kunye nokhuphelo loxwebhu lwakho lobunikazi. Okanye tsalela i-SASSA ku-0800 60 10 11 uze ubacele ukuba balungiselele ukutyelela ekhaya.
